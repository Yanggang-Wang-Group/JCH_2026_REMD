# JCH_2026_REMD

Raw data for "Replica Exchange across Reaction Coordinates for Data-Efficient Machine Learning Potentials " published in xxx

The complexity and heterogeneity of catalytic interfaces under realistic reaction conditions pose a fundamental challenge for conventional ab initio molecular dynamics (AIMD) in resolving coupled reaction mechanisms. Critical phenomena, including solvent reorganization, dynamic adsorbate coverage, ion fluctuations and competing reaction pathways, remain severely undersampled. Overcoming this limitation requires accurate machine learning interatomic potentials (MLIPs) capable of exploring multidimensional free energy landscapes. Herein, we developed an efficient MLIP training strategy employing replica exchange molecular dynamics (REMD) across both temperature and reaction coordinate dimensions, enabling efficient traversal of the training set along various reaction pathways and yielding a MLIP that faithfully reproduces reaction paths in a manner similar to reactive force fields. Using this MLIP, we performed nanometer and nanosecond-scale explicit solvent simulations of heterogeneous catalytic reactions in DFT-level accuracy. Applied to the Volmer step of the hydrogen evolution reaction (HER) and the oxygen reduction reaction (ORR) on Pt, the framework effectively captures the corresponding reaction pathways under realistic solvated. This workflow provides a critical framework for resolving coverage and solvent-dependent electrocatalytic mechanisms within multidimensional, complex reaction landscapes.
